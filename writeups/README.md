# CTF Writeups

A collection of my ctf challenge solutions and writups, organized by platform

## My Approach / On the Use of AI

I like to believe that I let my curiosity guide me, rather than my desire to "finish" the challenge.

I approach CTFs as learning exercises.

I treat AI tools like they're a teacher walking me through the exercise. It's my reponsibility to follow along and ask questions relevant to my understanding.

A few years ago I would have had to skim multiple blog posts, articles, etc. Now I can use an AI to distill the relevant info for me.

There are occasions when I'm in the mood to pursue a more "purist" route of classic web search and content skimming, or when I feel that the AI is missing something, and then I'll get down in the trenches again.

There is definitely a discussion to be had about the value of manually skimming a lot of semi-related content that isn't necessarily exactly what one is looking for and how that contributes to the learning process.  But for the most part, I like to find the answers specific to the questions I'm asking, so I use the tools available, and that means AI a lot of the time.

### Things I generally ask the AI

- how did you know to do that?
- how could a ctf participant reasonably be expected to know to do that?
- how can I recognize this in the future?

### Writing code
I am most familiar with the Python coding language. The Python course was one of my favorite parts of my degree program. I've written my own code before.

That being said, for most CTF processes, I will have AI generate code, either through specific prompts, or by following the AI suggestions. When I am unfamiliar with what the code is doing, I'll ask the AI to break it down for me and explain.
